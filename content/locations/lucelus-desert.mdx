# The Lucelus Desert

> **Work in progress.** This entry is part of the Character Origins region registry and will be expanded with full lore.

The **Lucelus Desert** is **vast**, lying **central** on the continent of Glaudire. To the **west** rise the **Peluc Peaks**; to the **east**, the **Dislow Heights** watch the dunes. **South** of the central sands lies **Tarpona**, a port city and trade hub; **northeast** lies another port, **Sarkir**.

## Ruins and Trade

Within the desert lie the great ancient ruins of **Bal'moral** and **Zal'fari** — names that draw scholars, treasure-hunters, and fools in equal measure.

The desert is also home to **Lucelean bandits**, caravans between **Tarpona** and **Sarkir**, and the **Free City of Tarkir** at its heart (see [The Free City of Tarkir](/lore/locations/tarkir-city)).

## Dangers in the Sands

<!-- CAMPAIGN SPOILER — omit from player-safe surfaces until spoiler system exists -->
Travelers report **smaller and medium-sized arcane monsters** already awake in the dunes, hunting trespassers and unwary sand-travelers. Deeper legends speak of **larger, undisturbed horrors** still dormant beneath the oldest ruins — a truth most maps do not print.
