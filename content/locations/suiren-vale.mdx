# Suiren Vale

> **Work in progress.** This entry is part of the Character Origins region registry and will be expanded with full lore.

Suiren Vale is a **beautiful open valley** along the **northern border of Lucia**. To the **east**, the **Aicem Peaks** rise; beyond them lies **Edezia**.

## The Aisarai and Aicem Rings

The Vale is home to the **Aisarai** people, practitioners of **Aicem Rings** — a tradition tied to harmony, restraint, and the living land.

Communities here are **nature-friendly**, favoring care for **animals** and the **environment** over expansion or conquest.

## Harmony Sanctuary

**Harmony Sanctuary** and its **Sanctuary monks** anchor much of the Vale’s spiritual life — a refuge for pilgrims, healers, and those fleeing the louder wars of neighboring realms.
