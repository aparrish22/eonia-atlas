# The Realm of Ikara

> **Work in progress.** This entry is part of the Character Origins region registry and will be expanded with full lore.

Ikara lies **northwest of the Lucelus Desert**, **past the Peluc Peaks**, and **north of Astia** — part of the **Western Realm** (alongside **Duris**, **Astia**, and **Reth**).

## A People Under Pressure

Ikarans have faced **recent and ongoing territorial wars against Astia**. They struggle to secure more land in part because **arcane monsters** roam the **Dreaded Wastes** — the blighted stretch of bad country **between Astia and Ikara**.

Despite hardship, Ikarans are broadly **friendly** and **diverse**, and they **welcome immigrants** from across the Western Realm and beyond.
