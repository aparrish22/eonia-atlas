# The Realm of Duris

> **Work in progress.** This entry is part of the Character Origins region registry and will be expanded with full lore.

Duris lies in the **far northwest** of Glaudire — **northwest of Astia**, ringed by **mountains and snow-filled plains**. It is largely a **winter habitat**: three seasons of winter and one period that splits **half spring and half fall**.

The realm was home to **dwarves of old**. Around **~1400 PT (post Trunam)**, its citizens — called **Drarvens** — are remarkably **short, stout, and hairy**, echoing their dwarven ancestors in build and temperament.

## Governance and Factions

The **King of the Duris Bloodline** holds court in the throne city of **Tyr Myhallis**.

Not all power bends to the crown: the **Kunrock Brothers and Sisters** have **separated themselves from Duris allegiance** and pursue their own priorities — a fracture the crown watches with cold patience.

## The Soldiers of the Wall

South of the realm, the **Soldiers of the Wall** enforce Duris’s **isolationist policy**, turning back **uninvited guests** and regulating what trade may pass under seal.

## Craft and Industry

Duris is known for **artificers**, **engineers**, and **miners** whose work supplies the realm’s sealed economy and its mountain-fast holds.
