# Blythgon Vale

> **Work in progress.** This entry is part of the Character Origins region registry and will be expanded with full lore.

Blythgon Vale is the **large valley and lake** that sits between **Lucia** and **Kircia**. High mountains guard the approaches, making the Vale difficult to traverse except by those who know its passes. The land is rich in **bounteous trees, lakes, and waterfalls** — a green heart between two magocratic and mercantile realms.

The people of the Vale are **Blythians**: natives whose culture favors **nature-arts**, quiet craft, and deliberate peace.

## Culture and Faith

Most who make the Vale their home are **druids and clerics** of gentle tradition, and many communities identify as **pacifists** — reluctant to take up arms except in the gravest defense of the Vale itself.

## The Peace Summit (~1200 PT)

Sometime around **~1200 PT (post Trunam)**, Blythians hosted a **peace summit** that marked a formal end to open hostility between **Lucia** and **Kircia**. The Vale’s reputation as neutral ground predates that treaty and has only deepened since.

See also: [The Realm of Lucia](/lore/locations/lucia-realm), [The Realm of Kircia](/lore/locations/kircia-realm).

## Geography

- **West / Southwest:** approaches from Kircia and the Blythgon corridor toward Dislow.
- **East:** Lucia’s northern marches.
- **Terrain:** mountain-ringed valley, central lake, dense forest.

> *The Vale does not conquer.*  
> *It waits, and grows.*
