# The Realm of Eldora

> **Work in progress.** This entry is part of the Character Origins region registry and will be expanded with full lore.

Eldora occupies its own **small continent** in the **Kipuan Sea**, **far southeast** of the Glaudire mainland. At its heart stands the **Great World Tree**, and the people who call themselves **Eldorans** organize much of their society around its roots, canopy, and wards.

## Long Life and the Guardians

Eldorans live **very long lives**. Those who survive past **500 years** — in Eonia, often measured in **yekzis** — lose most of their day-to-day autonomy by custom: society **encourages** such elders to dedicate their remaining centuries to the **defense and maintenance** of the Great World Tree.

Many of the longest-lived Eldorans at the tree now serve as its **guardians**, a role part honor, part obligation, part arcane necessity.
