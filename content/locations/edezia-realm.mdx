# The Realm of Edezia

> **Work in progress.** This entry is part of the Character Origins region registry and will be expanded with full lore.

Edezia sits in the **far northeast** of the Glaudire continent — **north of Lucia** and **east of Harmony Sanctuary** in **Suiren Vale**. The realm has **persisted across the ages**, layered with **ruins** and memories older than many neighboring crowns.

## Monster Hunters

Edezia is home to the **most skilled monster hunters of Glaudire**. By necessity as much as pride, they stand between the realm’s blighted wilds and the softer lands beyond.

The realm is **unintentionally isolated**: its people are too busy **holding back horrifying monsters** to court distant diplomacy, yet travelers owe their safer roads partly to Edezian blades.
