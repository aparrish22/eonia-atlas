# The Kipuan Islands

> **Work in progress.** This entry is part of the Character Origins region registry and will be expanded with full lore.

The **Kipuan Islands** lie **out at sea, southwest of the Glaudire continent** — a broken archipelago of **jungle isles**, hidden coves, and reefs that swallow careless hulls.

## Sea-Folk and Pirates

**Pirates** and **sea-farers** inhabit many of these islands, living by charter, raid, and rumor. Profit and peril share the same tide.

## Hidden Sites

**Many unknown sites** hide within the islands’ jungles and sea-caves — ruins, wrecks, and stranger things — leaving **plunder and discovery** to those bold enough to sail without a crown’s blessing.
