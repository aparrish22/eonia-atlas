# The Realm of Astia

> **Work in progress.** This entry is part of the Character Origins region registry and will be expanded with full lore.

Astia sits in the **central-west** reaches of Glaudire, where the **Peluc Peaks** form a stony, snow-capped boundary against the **Lucelus Desert** to the east. Its identity is shaped by **martial culture**, **duty**, and a heritage of **chivalry and nationalism** that runs deep through both its highborn houses and its rural levies.

## Geography and Borders

- **East / Northeast:** Peluc Peaks separate Astia from the Lucelus Desert and the realms beyond.
- **Southwest:** The long peninsula of **Reth** lies south-west across the Peluc range, joined to Astia by a long-standing alliance.
