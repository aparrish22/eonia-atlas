# The Free City of Tarkir

> **Work in progress.** This entry is part of the Character Origins region registry and will be expanded with full lore.

**Tarkir** is the **Free City** at the **center of the Lucelus Desert**, built beside a **great oasis lake**. It is the desert’s practical capital — neutral enough to broker passage, bold enough to profit from risk.

## Expedition Economy

Tarkir’s wealth comes largely from **hosting and supplying expeditions** into the ruins of **Zal'fari** and **Bal'moral**, far to the **south** across the sands. Guides, water-rights, maps, and licensed salvage all flow through its markets.
