# The Realm of Reth

> **Work in progress.** This entry is part of the Character Origins region registry and will be expanded with full lore.

Reth occupies a **large peninsula in the southwest** of Glaudire, reached by traveling south-west of the **Lucelus Desert** and **past the Peluc Peaks** mountain range. Its position — half guarded by sea, half by mountains — has historically given it both insulation and a fragile self-reliance.

## A Realm in Crisis

The **King and Queen of Reth have been assassinated.** The **rightful heir is missing — or dead** — and in the resulting vacuum, **politicians, generals, and old nobility** are circling, each maneuvering for a share of power.

- **Divided political ideologies** mark every council chamber.
- **Rebellion** and **secessionist** movements have emerged in outlying provinces.
- Talk of **civil war** is no longer hypothetical in many courts.

## Alliances

Reth maintains a long-standing **alliance with Astia** (see [The Realm of Astia](/lore/locations/astia-realm)). Astian envoys remain present, and Astian arms have quietly reinforced certain border holdings — but Reth’s internal collapse limits how much external help can stabilize the realm without becoming an occupation.

## Geography and Borders

- **Northeast:** Peluc Peaks and, beyond them, the Lucelus Desert.
- **East:** Astia, along the alliance border.
- **Coast:** A long peninsular shoreline.
