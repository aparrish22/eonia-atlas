# The Realm of Lucia

> **Work in progress.** This entry is part of the Character Origins region registry and will be expanded with full lore.

Lucia spans the **central-east** of Glaudire — beginning east of the **Lucelus Desert**, climbing through the **Dislow Heights**, and reaching all the way to the coastline along the **Kipua Sea**. On the southern borders of Lucia lies the **Blythgon Vale**, the valley that is shared with Kircia.

## Governance: The High Houses

Lucia is ruled — or, more accurately, **contested** — by a constellation of **High Houses**, each magocratic in tradition and increasingly autocratic in practice. A quiet political shift is underway: several Houses are positioning themselves to declare full **Sovereign House** status, blurring the line between noble family and independent power.

- **House Wars** are mostly fought through intrigue, marriage, magical patronage, and economic pressure — rarely open battle.
- **Rivalries** between Houses make excellent backstory ground: feuds, exiles, fostered children, blood-debts, and sworn vendettas.

## Military Presence

Elite **Lucian** units are stationed at **Dislow Garrison** alongside Kircian forces, watching the Lucelus Desert and maintaining the joint defensive line through the Heights. The arrangement is uneasy but pragmatic — both realms benefit from a stable frontier.

See also: [Dislow Garrison](/lore/locations/dislow-garrison) and [The Realm of Kircia](/lore/locations/kircia-realm).

## Geography and Borders

- **West:** Lucelus Desert — Lucia’s hold begins where the dunes give way to the Dislow Heights.
- **Southwest:** Borderlands shared with Kircia.
- **North:** Blythgon Vale.
- **East:** Coastline along the Kipua Sea.
