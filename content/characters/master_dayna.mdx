# Master Dayna

Master Dayna is the author of the study "Realm of the Mind". She is known for her craft on mapping out the complex architecture of the mind with the ability to sense in others many may never realize about themselves. **Brinian deals with the mind**: quiet and loud, soft and hard, spoken and unspoken, the trained mind can achieve great things.

Dayna is known for his **patience and unblinking focus**. He can listen to a student’s stumbling explanation longer than most Masters, then answer with a single, well-aimed question that forces them to reconsider everything they thought they understood.

## Appearance

- With a thin figure, her dark hazel eyes complement her narrow face underneath the darker shadows of her hair.
- Wears silken robes of red trimming and dark fabric over her dark buttoned dress.
- For her travels or classrooms, her brown hair is always tied up to a lazy bun; some days shining with darker red undertones as if auburn. During formal events, she sets curls and waves to her hair, often times capturing the room the moment she enters them.
- Following the clicks of her heels her hometown's long, dark finely-crafted master robes flow elegantly matching her every step with purpose and grace.

## Personality

- **Observant** – Dayna is the most observant master out of the entire academy. [[Master Quince]] often asks her to accompany him on his visits to the city council for her ability to see more than meets the eye.
- **Fostering** – She prioritizes her students' growth. She looks after them and prepares them for their coming years of becoming an arcanist of magic.
- **Thoughtful** – She gives mostly everything a second thought, careful to indulge the most quietest and under-looked thought. Except for her attire and prowess in formal balls or business inquiries. That is where her confidence blooms.
